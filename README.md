A broadsheet front page for Saturday 21 November 2026 — one day, the whole
world. Dark navy and gold design; one dominant headline plus four story
panels and a bottom ticker.

## The stories

1. **Missiles fall on Poland** — Russia fires 400+ drones and 110 missiles
   at Ukraine; Kyiv bridges hit, at least 8 killed.
2. **AI rout sinks stocks** — S&P 500 falls 3.7% into correction.
3. **Myanmar guilty of genocide** — World Court rules for the Rohingya.
4. **COP31 talks deadlock** — Fossil fuel row stalls the Antalya summit.
5. **Cancer pill breakthrough** — Tumors vanish in 1 of 12 patients.
6. **Peru quake 6.9** shakes the Andes.
7. **FBI database hacked.**
8. **UK and EU sign post-Brexit deals.**

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node.js, standard library only) copies the pre-made front-page
image `entry.png` to the output directory (`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` or
`/output/entry.png`), deterministically and byte-identically.
